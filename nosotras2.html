For Those Who Walk Beyond the Noise

You were invited — your presence is all that is needed.

This is a space for those who speak softly, think deeply, and feel demasiado. For those who want to belong, not to a crowd, but to a current. Welcome to Pocas Nosotras — a shared journey where ideas, silence, and symbolism intertwine.

Sectarias

We are thinkers, questioners, and feelers. Bound by breath and text, every Sectaria is equal, with their voice contributing to the quiet pulse of the collective. Those who linger, contribute, and return are known as Altar Sectarias, the ones who hold the space.

The Ceremony

There are no rules. No rituals — unless you make them. Only symbols, silences, and the quiet knowing of Pocas Nosotras. The ceremony begins when you arrive. Your presence, your thoughts, your essence — they matter. This is where belonging isn’t about a crowd. It’s about a current.
